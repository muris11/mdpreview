Privacy policy

MDPreview Center is built so that the default answer to "does this leave my browser?" is no. This page states exactly what does, what does not, and the two features that opt you out of that default.

Last updated 26 October 2026. The app has no accounts, no server-side database of your documents and no analytics cookies.

The short version

What you do Status Details
Typing, preview, export, auto-save Entirely local Stored in localStorage on your device only.
Full share link (#md=…) Never sent to a server Lives in the URL fragment, which browsers exclude from HTTP requests.
Short link (/s/…) Leaves your browser The compressed document is stored on a public paste service for up to 365 days.
Pasted images Leaves your browser Forwarded through /api/upload to a public image host; the returned URL is public and embedded in your Markdown.
Deployed site traffic Aggregate stats only A Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon loads on the deployed site. It is injected by Cloudflare, is cookieless and is not part of this repository.

What stays on your device

  • Your document. The Markdown you write and the title you give it are saved to localStorage under the mdpreview key, debounced so it does not write on every keystroke.
  • Your preferences. Theme, view mode, word wrap, scroll sync and split ratio are stored the same way.
  • Nothing else. There is no account, no profile, no server-side session and no identifier attached to you.

Clearing your browser's site data removes the document permanently — there is no copy elsewhere to delete.

How share links work

The full link serialises your document, compresses it with LZ-String and places it after # in the URL. The fragment is never included in an HTTP request, so opening or hosting the link does not transmit the content to us — only to whoever you send it to.

The short link is different by design. To give you a URL short enough for chat apps, the compressed document is uploaded to dpaste.com, a free paste service, and expires after 365 days. Treat short links like any other public paste: do not use them for secrets.

Pasted images

Browsers cannot upload directly to free image hosts because of CORS, so pasted images are posted to this site's own /api/upload function, which forwards the file to catbox.moe and returns a public URL. The upload endpoint validates the file type, rejects anything larger than about 4.2 MB of base64 data and does not keep a copy.

If your images are sensitive, host them yourself and paste the resulting URLs into your Markdown instead.

Cookies and analytics

MDPreview Center sets no cookies of its own. The deployed site loads a Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon for aggregate page-view counts; it is cookieless, does not identify individuals and is not part of this repository's code.

Third parties

  • dpaste.com — stores short-link documents until they expire.
  • catbox.moe — hosts pasted images.
  • Google Fonts — serves the Inter and JetBrains Mono web fonts.
  • Cloudflare — serves the site and, on the deployed version, the analytics beacon.

Each receives only what is needed to do its job. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting something new, this page will say so before it ships. Substantive edits are dated above.
